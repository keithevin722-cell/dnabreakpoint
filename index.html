Emerging Therapeutic Vectors & Delivery Platforms

Summary for orientation only; verify payload limits and approval status against current primary sources.

HPV Pseudovirions (PsVs)

Max Payload Capacity
8 kb (suited to large CRISPR-Cas9 complexes)
Tissue Tropism / Target
Epithelial tissues, genetic skin disorders, mucosal membranes
Clinical Status
Investigational / pre-clinical delivery vehicle

Adeno-Associated Virus (AAV)

Max Payload Capacity
~4.7 kb
Tissue Tropism / Target
Systemic organs, central nervous system, retina, liver
Clinical Status
FDA-approved (e.g., Luxturna, Zolgensma)

Lentiviral Vectors

Max Payload Capacity
~8-10 kb
Tissue Tropism / Target
Ex vivo hematopoietic stem cells, T-cells (CAR-T therapy)
Clinical Status
FDA-approved / active clinical pipelines

Oncolytic Viruses

Max Payload Capacity
Not payload-limited; mechanism: selective replication inside tumor cells causing cell lysis
Tissue Tropism / Target
Solid tumors, advanced melanomas (e.g., T-VEC)
Clinical Status
FDA-approved / combination immuno-oncology
